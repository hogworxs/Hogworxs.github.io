Delete my data

Range Forged · HOG WORX · Last updated November 16, 2026

Range Forged keeps your rifles, sessions, loads, settings and other range records primarily on your phone. No account is required for the current testing build.

Delete app records

Delete individual records inside Range Forged, or uninstall the app to remove its private app data. Before uninstalling, save anything you want to keep.

Recently deleted and in-app safety copies

Deleting an individual record first moves it to Settings → Recently deleted. It remains there until you empty Recently deleted. To permanently remove it from the active app and Recently deleted, empty that section. The app also retains up to five internal safety copies, which may still include deleted records. Older copies are replaced as newer ones are made, typically around once per day, so there is no guaranteed maximum retention time if the app is not used. To remove them immediately, open Settings → Backup and restore → Safety copies and delete them. Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes Recently deleted and these internal safety copies, but not files exported to shared storage.

Delete backup files

Uninstalling does not necessarily delete exported files. In version 2.0.1 and later, open your phone's Files app, navigate to Internal storage → Documents → RangeForged, and delete any automatic backup files you no longer want. Also delete any backups you previously saved or shared to other folders, email, or cloud services. Earlier versions may have manually exported backups in locations you selected.

Request deletion of server-held information

The AI coach is disabled in the current testing release. If you used a coach-enabled version and want associated server-held usage information deleted, email brandon.dean@hogworxs.com with the subject “Range Forged Data Deletion Request.” We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days. Coach answers are retained for up to 24 hours and coach question counts for the current billing period. Because the app does not currently display its anonymous coach identifier, we may be unable to match an email request to a specific record; an in-app deletion option is planned before the coach is enabled. We may ask for information needed to locate the relevant anonymous record; please do not email firearm or shooting records unless specifically needed. We cannot delete copies you independently shared with other services.

For more information, read our privacy policy.
