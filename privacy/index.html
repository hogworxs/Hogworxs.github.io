Range Forged privacy policy

HOG WORX · Last updated November 16, 2026

Range Forged is an offline-first Android range and precision shooting app. You can use its core features without an account. We do not show ads, use analytics or advertising trackers, or sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Rifles, loads, optics, DOPE, stages, load tests, sessions, timer results, notes, photos, and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive your shooting records automatically. You may choose to export or share backup files yourself.

In version 2.0.0, use Settings → Backup and restore to save a backup file. Starting with version 2.0.1, if installed, Range Forged also creates automatic backup files in your phone's Documents/RangeForged folder (up to three recent files). This is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings → Backup and restore. These files remain after uninstalling the app and may be accessible to other apps with access to your Documents folder. Backups on the same phone do not protect against loss, damage, or factory reset; you can copy a backup elsewhere yourself. Android's Google cloud app backup is disabled.

Location and live weather

With your permission, the app uses location locally for magnetic declination, GPS altitude and distances to weather stations. For live weather, your position rounded to approximately 1 km is sent to the U.S. National Weather Service at api.weather.gov to retrieve nearby conditions. When enabled, live weather is requested when the Rifle screen opens and refreshed approximately every 10 minutes while that screen remains open. The weather service also receives your device's internet IP address as part of the request. HOG WORX does not receive or store your location. You can disable live weather or location access and enter conditions manually.

Camera and microphone

The microphone is used for shot detection and audio timing checks. The camera is used for the Live Target feature and, through the phone's camera app, to take target photos and record draw-coach videos. Those photos and videos stay on the phone. Audio, camera images and video are processed on your device and are not uploaded by these features. Photos or videos included in a backup may be copied or shared if you choose to export that backup.

AI coach

The AI coach is not enabled in the current public testing build. When enabled in a future version, asking the coach will send your question, relevant rifle/load and shooting calculations shown in the app, and an anonymous identifier to a HOG WORX server hosted by Cloudflare. The request is then processed by Anthropic under its privacy terms. The coach is designed not to send your name, email address or precise location. Coach answers are retained on the server for up to 24 hours for retry handling; question counts are retained for the current billing period. The app does not currently expose the anonymous coach identifier, so requests to locate a specific record may not be possible until an in-app deletion control is available. We will update this notice if its operation changes before activation.

Purchases

Subscriptions and Google Play Billing are not enabled in the current test build. If introduced, Google Play will handle payment information and this policy will be updated to explain subscription verification and any purchase-token processing before billing launches.

Deleting data

Deleting records in the app moves them to Settings → Recently deleted, where they remain until you empty Recently deleted. The app also retains up to five in-app safety copies, which can include deleted records until newer copies replace them (typically around one per day), or until you remove them in Settings → Backup and restore → Safety copies. Uninstalling removes the app's private data, but does not automatically remove manual backups or, from version 2.0.1, automatic backups in Documents/RangeForged. You must delete those files separately. See Delete My Data for instructions and how to request deletion of any server-held coach information.

Network security and external links

App network connections use encrypted HTTPS; unencrypted app traffic is blocked. State-law reference links open in your phone's browser.

Children

Range Forged is a firearms training tool intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app develops and will update the date above. Contact brandon.dean@hogworxs.com with privacy questions.
